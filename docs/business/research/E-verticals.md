# Track E: Transferability, worked examples beyond robotics

Track E of the ShiftLead business model sprint. Researched 2026-10-03, time box about 20 minutes.

Claim under test: "same core, swap the adapter." The core is the event engine, ticket state machine,
people/travel assignment, trust gate and briefs. An adapter is data connectors, detectors, vocabulary and a
qualification taxonomy.

Conventions (per BRIEF.md):
- **Verified** = I opened the page or PDF and saw the number or quote.
- **Unverified** = seen only in a search snippet or secondhand.
- **CALC** = my own calculation, inputs shown.
- **Judgment** = my design or ranking opinion, not a sourced fact. Incumbent tool names are from domain
  knowledge and were **not verified in this sprint**, unless a finding number is cited.

## 3. Recommendation for the "Swap the adapter. Keep the core." slide

Scoring is my judgment, 1-3 per criterion, anchored on the findings above:

| Vertical | (a) People/travel loop vividness | (b) Size | (c) Why-local strength | Total | Tie-breakers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hospitals HTM | 3: multi-campus (69.7% in systems, H3); 89% of repairers work outside hospitals and travel (H2), the same shape as robotics field engineers | 2: 6,100 hospitals, 71,800 repairers | 3: HIPAA (H5), $7.42M per breach (H6), CMS maintenance rule (H4) | **8** | Best fit for a "100% local" judging panel |
| Grocery refrigeration | 3: 4-hour discard clock (G6); EPA 30-day repair (G2); ALD alert → "must inspect" (G3) | 3: ~37,900 stores (CALC, G4), 440,900 HVACR techs (G1) | 1: no data-sensitivity rule found | **7** | Trust gate maps onto EPA's mandatory verification tests; ALD makes a detector feed mandatory by Jan 1 2027 |
| Utilities & telecom | 2: storms, crews, mutual aid, but OMS/ADMS + mobile workforce tools already cover much of this loop (judgment, not verified) | 3: 389,700 workers (CALC), $44B-$80B/yr outage cost | 2: NERC CIP / CPNI, with caveats | **7** | Needs crew assignment, not one-person assignment; incumbents are deepest here |
| EV charging | 3: unattended sites; 97% uptime ≈ 11 days/port/yr budget (CALC); certified-tech filter (E3) | 2: 82,581 sites / 260,486 ports; no workforce count | 1: status must be public by law (E2) | **6** | Cleanest adapter (OCPP/OCPI, an open standard); crisp numeric SLA; NEVI politically uncertain |

**Recommended slide (ranked):**
1. **Hospitals / health systems (HTM).** The strongest why-local story, plus a regulated maintenance loop and
   multi-campus travel. It mirrors the robotics wedge: most medical equipment repairers are vendor field engineers
   who travel. Headline numbers: 6,100 hospitals, 71,800 repairers, $7.42M per healthcare breach.
2. **Grocery refrigeration.** The most vivid clock ("41°F for >4 hours → discard"). EPA now mandates the
   detector (ALD by 1/1/2027) and the verification step (initial + follow-up tests), which map onto the
   detect → fix → verify loop. Headline: 440,900 HVACR techs; EPA 30-day repair rule.
3. **Optional third: EV charging.** Show it as the cleanest "swap the adapter" proof (OCPP in, 97% uptime
   clock, certified-tech filter), not as a why-local story.

Keep **utilities/telecom** for Q&A as the scale answer: 389,700 field workers and $44B-$80B a year in outage cost.
Its why-local case is good, but incumbents there are most mature.

Slide-safe wording: "Same core: event engine, tickets, who-goes + travel time, trust gate, briefs. New adapter:
connectors + detectors + vocabulary + qualifications." Avoid any "% of code reused" number; none was measured.
