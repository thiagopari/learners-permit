## Caveats for the pitch
1. **None of these companies is a ShiftLead customer.** "Our first customers are a short drive from this room" would be false as a statement of fact. Say "target" or "natural first customers".
2. **Most of the job postings are closed.**
   - Closed: Locus Director (removed Jan 2026), Berkshire Grey (removed Nov 2025), Vecna and Pickle (6+ months old, closed).
   - Currently listed: only the Locus onsite support openings.
   - Say "have hired" or "employ", not "are hiring right now".
3. **Distance:** the only sourced figure is Boston Dynamics' "approximately 12 miles outside Boston". "A short drive" is fine as rhetoric for Wilmington, Waltham, Bedford and Charlestown, but I did not verify mileage from the venue.

## Recommended pitch sentence (supported by verified facts only)
"Our natural first customers are a short drive from this room. Symbotic in Wilmington says about half its employees work at customer sites installing and maintaining its systems. Locus in Wilmington, Vecna in Waltham, Berkshire Grey in Bedford and Pickle in Charlestown have all hired field technicians who spend 40 to 100 percent of their time traveling to customer robots."

Inputs: 40-60% (Locus), 75-100% (Vecna), 90% (Berkshire Grey), 75% (Pickle). Boston Dynamics is left out of the sentence because its field-service evidence is unverified. Add it only if someone opens a live Boston Dynamics posting.
