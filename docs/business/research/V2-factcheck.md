## Notes for the slide writer

1. **Dell vs DGX Spark are different products.** NVIDIA's $4,699 is the DGX Spark Founders Edition MSRP. The forum post itself says OEM GB10 pricing is set by the OEM. Dell's $9,007 includes a 4TB self-encrypting drive. A defensible slide line: "Dell Pro Max with GB10 from $6,177 (1TB) to $9,007 (4TB) on Dell.com, 2026-10-03; NVIDIA DGX Spark MSRP $4,699". The two Dell URLs are not independent sources: the business-store URL redirects to the same product page.
2. **Electricity:** July is the highest commercial month so far in 2026 (monthly range 13.51 to 14.53 ¢). For an *annual* power-cost line, the rolling 12-month figure (13.85 ¢) or YTD (13.97 ¢) is more defensible than the July peak. At 13.85 ¢ (CALCULATION, 8,760 h): 280 W ≈ $340/yr, 140 W ≈ $170/yr. Either way the conclusion holds: about $165 to $360 per site per year.
3. **Cheapest Qwen tokens come from an fp4 endpoint on a small host (Darkbloom).** Mainstream fp8 hosts (DeepInfra, AkashML, Venice) charge $0.10 input and $0.90 to $1.00 output.
4. Unverified context, not checked against an official source: an NVIDIA forum user post (2026-06-11, "GX10 Price Increase 3499 -> 3999") says ASUS's GB10 box rose to $3,999 on Amazon. Don't put this on a slide without checking the vendor page.
